## Two different philosophies

Bullpadel's range leans toward stiffer, more aggressive rackets built around Spanish
professional players' feedback — expect firmer faces and less give. Head's range,
influenced by their tennis heritage, tends toward more balanced, comfort-oriented
builds across similar price points.

## On court differences

You'll typically feel more vibration and more raw power from a Bullpadel racket at a
given weight, versus a softer, more controlled feel from a comparable Head model. Neither
is objectively better — it comes down to whether you want the racket to amplify your
swing or smooth it out.

## Our take

Try before you commit if you can. If you can't demo either, lean Bullpadel if you already
play aggressively and Head if you're still finding your game style.
