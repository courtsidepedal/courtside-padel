## Temperature is the biggest factor

Leaving a racket in a hot car is the single most common cause of early delamination and
face cracking — the resins in the carbon face degrade faster under repeated heat
exposure. Store your racket at room temperature whenever possible.

## Grip and overgrip

Replace your overgrip every few weeks of regular play, not just when it visibly wears
out — a degraded grip changes how you hold the racket and can quietly affect your
technique before it affects your hand.

## What doesn't matter much

Cleaning the face after every session with anything beyond a damp cloth isn't
necessary, and aggressive scrubbing can wear down surface texture that generates spin.
