## Shape matters more than the marketing

Padel rackets come in three broad shapes: round, teardrop, and diamond. Round shapes
are the most forgiving and the right default for almost every new player. Diamond
shapes push power higher up the racket but punish mishits hard — save those for once
your technique is solid.

## Weight and balance

Lighter rackets (350-365g) with low balance are easier to control and less tiring for a
new player's arm. You don't need a heavy, high-balance racket to hit hard yet — that
comes from technique, not racket weight.

## What to ignore at first

Marketing terms like "pro-level carbon" or specific foam densities matter far less at
beginner level than shape and weight. Don't pay a premium for specs you won't notice
the benefit of for a year or more.

## What to actually buy

A round-shaped, low-balance racket in the €120-180 range is the sweet spot for a first
"real" racket, once you've outgrown a basic starter set.
