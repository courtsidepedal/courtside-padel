## First impressions

The Speed Pro 2025 sits in the gap between pure power rackets and pure control
rackets — its teardrop shape splits the difference between a diamond's raw smash
power and a round racket's forgiveness, and owner reviews suggest Head has landed
that balance well.

## On court

The standout feature reviewers point to is the Auxetic 2.0 foam construction, which is
widely described as giving a noticeably softer, more comfortable feel at contact than
older Head power rackets. The hybrid carbon-fibreglass face still generates strong pace
on smashes, just without the harsh, unforgiving sensation of a stiffer diamond-shaped
racket on off-centre hits.

## Who it's for

Upper-intermediate and advanced players who want to attack but aren't yet — or don't
want to be — the kind of player who only wins points on pure smash power. If your game
mixes aggression with placement, this is built for that mix rather than forcing you to
pick a lane.
