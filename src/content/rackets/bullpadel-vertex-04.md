## First impressions

The Vertex 04 sits at the top of Bullpadel's power line, and it plays like it. The diamond
shape pushes the sweet spot high toward the tip, which means every well-struck smash
carries real weight — but it also means the margin for error shrinks compared to a
round or teardrop shape.

## On court

Cross-court shots pick up noticeably more spin thanks to the rougher 3K carbon face,
which is useful against opponents who struggle with a low, skidding ball. Defensively
it's less forgiving: balls off the frame or low on the face lose a lot of control, so this
isn't a racket to reach for if your technique is still developing.

## Who it's for

If you're already an aggressive, attack-first player with a reliable smash, the Vertex 04
gives you more ceiling. If you're still building consistency, look at a round or teardrop
shape first — this one will expose technique gaps rather than cover for them.
