## First impressions

Round-shaped rackets exist to be forgiving, and the Motion Pro does that job well. The
sweet spot is large and centred, so mishits at the net or on defensive lobs don't punish
you the way they would on a diamond shape.

## On court

Where this racket earns its "control" label is in touch shots — bandejas, viboras, and
soft drop shots near the net all feel predictable. It won't out-power a dedicated
attacking racket in a straight smash battle, but most rallies aren't won on smashes
alone.

## Who it's for

Genuinely most players. If you're new to padel, this is a sensible first "real" racket once
you've outgrown a starter set. If you're intermediate and unsure whether you're an
attacker or defender yet, this buys you time to find out.
