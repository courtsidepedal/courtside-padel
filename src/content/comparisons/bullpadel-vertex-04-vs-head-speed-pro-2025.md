## The short version

Both of these are advanced-level rackets, but they sit at different points on the
power-versus-forgiveness spectrum. The Vertex 04 is a diamond built purely for
maximum smash power. The Speed Pro 2025 is a teardrop hybrid — still genuinely
powerful, but with more built-in margin for error.

## Where they differ most

The Vertex 04's sweet spot sits higher and smaller, rewarding clean technique with the
hardest smash in this comparison — but it punishes off-centre contact more than either
of the other two shapes on the site. The Speed Pro 2025 spreads its sweet spot wider
via its teardrop shape and softer Auxetic 2.0 construction, trading a small amount of
outright power for a racket that's more forgiving to actually play with over a full match.

## Bottom line

Pick the Vertex 04 if pure smash power is what you're optimizing for and your technique
is already reliable. Pick the Speed Pro 2025 if you want a racket that still hits hard but
gives you more room to miss.
