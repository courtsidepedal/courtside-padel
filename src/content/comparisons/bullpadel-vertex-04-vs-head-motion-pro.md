## The short version

These two sit at opposite ends of the shape spectrum — the Vertex 04 is a diamond
built for power, the Motion Pro is a round racket built to forgive. Which one is "better"
depends entirely on your game style, not your skill level alone.

## Where they differ most

The Vertex 04's sweet spot sits higher and smaller, rewarding clean technique with more
smash power. The Motion Pro spreads its sweet spot wider and lower, trading some
outright power for consistency on defence and at the net.

## Bottom line

Pick the Vertex 04 if you already attack with confidence. Pick the Motion Pro if you want
one racket that won't punish you while your game is still developing.
