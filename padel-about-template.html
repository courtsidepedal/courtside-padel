How we test

Every racket earns its score

01

Real match play

Every racket is used in actual matches, not just a few practice swings, over a minimum two-week period.

02

Multiple skill levels

Tested by players ranging from intermediate to advanced, so the review reflects more than one perspective.

03

Bought at retail price

We purchase rackets ourselves wherever possible, the same way you would — no manufacturer loaner units.

04

Re-tested over time

Scores get revisited as rackets wear in, not just judged on a first impression out of the box.

Our commitment

We earn money through affiliate links — if you buy a racket through one of our links, we may get a small commission at no extra cost to you.

That commission never determines a score. We've given low scores to rackets from every major brand, including ones we're affiliated with. If a racket isn't good, we say so.
