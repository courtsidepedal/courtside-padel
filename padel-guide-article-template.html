How to Choose Your First Padel Racket Without Wasting Money

8 min read·Updated Aug 2026

Most beginners buy the wrong racket — not because they pick a bad brand, but because they optimize for the wrong spec entirely. Here's what actually matters when you're starting out, in the order it actually matters.

Why racket choice matters

A racket built for advanced power players will punish you as a beginner — smaller sweet spot, harsher vibration, less forgiveness on off-center hits. The gear that pros use isn't the gear you should start with.

The single biggest beginner mistake: buying the racket a pro uses instead of the racket built for your level.

Weight: the #1 factor

Lighter rackets (355–365g) are easier to control and reduce arm fatigue while you're still building technique. Heavier rackets add power you don't need yet, at the cost of control you do.

Shape explained

Round shapes have the biggest sweet spot and the most forgiveness — this is what you want starting out. Diamond shapes (built for power) come later, once your technique catches up.

Balance for beginners

Low or medium balance keeps the racket's weight closer to your hand, making it easier to react quickly and control your shots. High-balance, head-heavy rackets are a later upgrade, not a starting point.

Our recommendation

If you're buying your first racket, prioritize in this order: weight, then shape, then balance. Brand and price come last — several beginner-friendly rackets in the €80-130 range outperform expensive advanced rackets for someone just starting out.
